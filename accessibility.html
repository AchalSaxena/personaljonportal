Accessibility Statement सुगम्यता विवरण

Standard: WCAG 2.1 & 2.2 Level AA • GIGW 3.0 • Last Updated: 25 October 2026

The Job Vacancy Intelligence Portal is committed to providing an accessible, inclusive, and barrier-free digital experience for all users, including individuals with visual, auditory, cognitive, and motor impairments.

Conformance Status & Standards

This portal is engineered to conform with:

WCAG 2.1 Level AA WCAG 2.2 Level AA GIGW 3.0 (STQC Requirements) ISO/IEC 40500:2012

Note on Certification: Internal automated unit and lint audits have verified compliance with Level AA criteria. Formal STQC (Standardisation Testing and Quality Certification) third-party certification is a regulatory process scheduled during official organizational evaluation.

Accessibility Features Implemented

  • Skip Navigation Links: Keyboard users can press Tab upon page load to immediately jump past headers to primary content or navigation.
  • Display Toolbar (Text Resizing): A-, A, and A+ buttons allow scalable font sizing without breaking responsive layouts or overflowing table containers.
  • High Contrast Mode: Instant yellow-on-black theme conforming to STQC specifications for high-contrast accessibility.
  • Keyboard Operability: All table filters, buttons, modal cards, links, and search inputs are fully operable via Tab, Enter, Space, and Escape keys.
  • Visible Keyboard Focus: High-contrast orange (#FF6B35) focus indicator with 2px offset ensures unambiguous active control identification.
  • Screen Reader Live Regions: Polite and assertive ARIA live regions announce dynamic table counts, filter updates, and status messages to assistive tech (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver).
  • Semantic HTML5 Structure: Strict landmark hierarchy with <header>, <main>, <nav>, <article>, and <footer>.
  • Target Size Compliance: Interactive controls satisfy WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 2.5.8 (minimum target area requirements).
  • Color Contrast Ratios: Text elements maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast against their respective backgrounds (large text at least 3:1).
  • Bilingual English–Hindi Interface: Native Devanagari font rendering with localized headers, controls, and announcements.

Known Limitations & Alternative Access

  • Third-Party PDFs: Downloadable external job circulars and notifications are published by respective hiring departments and may vary in accessibility. The candidate's verified CV is provided with verified size [PDF, 110 KB] and accessible text representation.
  • External Recruitment Portals: When following external application links (e.g. C-DAC, STPI, SAIL, RITES), users are transitioned to external platforms governed by their respective accessibility standards.

Feedback and Grievance Mechanism

We welcome user feedback on digital accessibility barriers. If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this portal, please submit an issue through:

  • The on-page Feedback form (available on the main dashboard).
  • The Contact Us page addressed to the Accessibility Coordinator.
  • Statutory resolution timeframe: Response acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within 5 working days.

रोजगार रिक्ति बुद्धिमत्ता पोर्टल सभी उपयोगकर्ताओं, विशेष रूप से दिव्यांगजनों के लिए एक समावेशी, सुलभ और बाधा-मुक्त डिजिटल अनुभव प्रदान करने हेतु प्रतिबद्ध है।

सुगम्यता अनुपालन स्थिति

यह पोर्टल निम्नलिखित अंतरराष्ट्रीय एवं राष्ट्रीय मानकों के अनुरूप विकसित किया गया है:

WCAG 2.1 Level AA WCAG 2.2 Level AA GIGW 3.0 (STQC आवश्यकताएं)

मुख्य सुगम्यता सुविधाएं

  • मुख्य सामग्री पर जाएं (Skip Link): कीबोर्ड से सीधे मुख्य भाग पर जाने की सुविधा।
  • फॉन्ट आकार नियंत्रण: A-, A, A+ बटनों द्वारा अक्षरों का आकार छोटा, सामान्य या बड़ा करने की सुविधा।
  • उच्च कंट्रास्ट मोड: दृष्टिबाधित उपयोगकर्ताओं हेतु विशेष पीला-काली (Yellow on Black) थीम।
  • स्क्रीन रीडर अनुकूल: ARIA लाइव रीजन्स एवं स्पष्ट लेबलिंग।
  • द्विभाषी इंटरफ़ेस: संपूर्ण पृष्ठों पर हिंदी एवं अंग्रेजी दोनों भाषाओं का पूर्ण समर्थन।

सुगम्यता संबंधी संपर्क

सुगम्यता संबंधी किसी भी कठिनाई के निवारण हेतु कृपया हमारे संपर्क पृष्ठ अथवा फीडबैक माध्यम का उपयोग करें।
