## ⏱️ Video Timeline & Scene-by-Scene Script

### Scene 1: Introduction & Executive Overview (0:00 - 0:45)

---

### Scene 2: Interactive Excel AutoFilters & Naya "Age Limit" Column (0:45 - 1:30)
**Screen Action:** Mouse cursor spreadsheet table par scroll karta hai. Naye **"Age Limit"** column aur **"Organization"** header ke **`▼`** icon par click hota hai. Excel popover khulta hai jisme search box, (Select All), aur count dikhta hai.

**🎙️ Voiceover (Hindi):**
> *"Ab aate hain hamare sabse powerful user feature par — **Excel-like Interactive AutoFilters**.*  
> *Table ke sabhi 12 columns par aapko bilkul Microsoft Excel jaisa AutoFilter dropdown button milta hai.*  
> *Customer ki demand par humne isme ek dedicated **Age Limit** column add kiya hai. Jaise hi aap `▼` icon par click karte hain, ek dynamic popover open hota hai jahan aap distinct age criteria — jaise 'Max 35 Years', 'Max 45 Years', ya 'Up to 55 Years' ko select ya search kar sakte hain.*  
> *Aap ek saath multiple columns par AND filter laga sakte hain, aur upar active filter chips ke through unhe ek click me clear kar sakte hain. Saath hi ye table mobile, tablet aur desktop par 100% touch-responsive hai."*

---

### Scene 3: Targeted Job Search Scheduler (1:30 - 2:15)
**Screen Action:** Scheduler Control Panel highlight hota hai. *"Start Job Search Scheduler"* button par click hota hai. Live activity ticker chalne lagta hai, timer start hota hai aur progress bar update hota hai. Phir *"Configured Websites"* modal khulta hai.

**🎙️ Voiceover (Hindi):**
> *"Hamara agla key component hai — **Manual Job Search Scheduler**.*  
> *Ye random internet surfing nahi karta; balki ye exclusively aapke configured websites text file — **job_websites.txt** ko read karta hai.*  
> *Yahan humne Indian Government aur PSU ke 9 premier portals configure kiye hain — jaise CDAC, STPI, Digital India Corporation (DIC), RITES, SAIL, aur NABARD.*  
> *Aap 'Start Job Search Scheduler' button click karke kabhi bhi targeted search run kar sakte hain. System har website ko safely scan karta hai, nayi vacancies extract karta hai, aur sabse important: **strict deduplication** engine ke through duplicate vacancies ko skip karke existing records ko preserve karta hai."*

---

### Scene 4: Smart Vacancy Parsing & CV Matching (2:15 - 3:00)
**Screen Action:** `+ Paste Vacancy Text` modal open hota hai. Ek raw government advertisement text paste kiya jata hai. System 1 second me extract karke Job Title, Organization, Qualification, Age Limit aur Match % calculate karta hai.

**🎙️ Voiceover (Hindi):**
> *"Kya aapke paas koi raw recruitment circular ya PDF text hai?*  
> *Aapko bas '+ Paste Vacancy Text' par click karna hai aur raw text paste karna hai.*  
> *Hamara smart algorithm automatically post title, department, qualification, experience aur age limit parse kar leta hai.*  
> *Iske baad ye Achal Saxena ke profile — M.Tech CSE, 10+ years experience, NeGD MeitY certifications aur Mumbai/Delhi priority location ke against real-time **Match Score (jaise 95% ya 98%)** calculate karke database me save kar deta hai."*

---

**Screen Action:** Audit Log modal dikhta hai, folder me `vacancies_log.csv` aur `sitemap.xml` highlight hote hain, aur terminal me `npm test` chalta hai (28/28 Passed).

> *Iski average API response latency sirf **2.8 milliseconds** hai.*  
>  
> *Agar aapko iska live demo chahiye ya koi customization require hai, toh aap hume directly contact kar sakte hain. Dhanyawaad!"*
