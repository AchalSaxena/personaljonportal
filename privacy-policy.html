Privacy Policy गोपनीयता नीति

Statutory Compliance: Information Technology Act, 2000 • Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 • GIGW 3.0 • Last Updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Job Vacancy Intelligence Portal collects, processes, and protects information in strict accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 (including the IT Reasonable Security Practices Rules) and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India.

1. Data Fiduciary Commitment & Principles

Under the DPDP Act 2023, the portal operator acts as a Data Fiduciary, committed to principles of purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limitation, and lawful grounds of processing. Personal data is collected exclusively upon voluntary submission by the user or as necessary for network security.

2. Information Collected

  • Usage & Telemetry Data: Browser user agent, requested URLs, and anonymized client IP address (used solely for DDoS prevention, rate limiting, and technical error diagnosis).
  • Feedback Submissions: Name (optional), email address (optional), category (Accessibility / Bug / Content / Suggestions), user rating (1-5), and voluntary feedback comments.
  • Local Preference Settings: Language selection (EN/HI), text-size scaling, and high-contrast display choices stored exclusively within the user's browser localStorage.

Explicit Exclusions: This portal does not collect sensitive personal data such as financial passwords, Aadhaar numbers, biometric identifiers, or financial payment details.

3. Purpose Limitation & Lawful Processing

  • To serve recruitment alerts, compute vacancy-candidate alignment scores, and display relevant job circulars.
  • To evaluate and rectify accessibility barriers or technical grievances submitted by users.
  • To safeguard server infrastructure against bot harvesting, directory brute-force, or malicious payload attacks.

4. Cookies and Tracking Mechanisms

This portal utilizes zero third-party tracking cookies, advertising network beacons, or behavioral profiling pixels. No cross-site user tracking is conducted.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

Personal information is never leased, traded, monetized, or shared with external commercial brokers. Information is disclosed only when legally mandated by Indian statutory authorities or court orders under applicable law.

6. Retention Schedules & Content Archival

In accordance with the portal's Content Archival Policy:

  • Feedback and grievance records are retained for 24 months to satisfy audit requirements under GIGW 3.0, after which they are securely purged.
  • System telemetry logs are retained for 90 days on a rolling purge basis.

7. Security Safeguards (Technical & Organisational)

  • HSTS & Modern TLS: Strict-Transport-Security enforced with 1-year duration and preload readiness.
  • Injection Protections: Parameterized SQLite queries preventing SQL injection vectors.
  • CORS Protection: Cross-origin write restrictions preventing cross-site state manipulation.
  • Payload Limiting & HTML Sanitization: Strict payload caps and automated HTML escaping to eliminate Stored XSS vectors.

8. Rights of Data Principals (DPDP Act 2023)

As a Data Principal under Indian law, you possess enforceable rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of your personal data processed by the portal.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request correction of inaccurate information or deletion of submitted feedback data.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Accessible resolution channel with a statutory 30-day timeline.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent at any time for voluntary feedback data by contacting the Grievance Officer.

9. Grievance Officer & Contact Information

  • Designated Officer: Achal Saxena
  • Designation: Portal Administrator & Technical Project Manager
  • Grievance Channel: Submit queries via the Contact Us Page or the interactive Feedback channel.
  • Statutory Response Timeline: Acknowledged within 48 hours; resolved within 30 days.

1. डेटा संरक्षण सिद्धांत

पोर्टल संचालक 'डेटा फिड्यूशरी' (Data Fiduciary) के रूप में कार्य करता है और डेटा न्यूनतमीकरण (Data Minimisation) तथा उद्देश्य परिसीमन के सिद्धांतों का कड़ाई से पालन करता है।

2. डेटा संग्रहण एवं उपयोग

  • फीडबैक डेटा: नाम (वैकल्पिक), ईमेल (वैकल्पिक) एवं सुझाव।
  • तकनीकी लॉग्स: सर्वर सुरक्षा हेतु नेटवर्क लॉग्स (90 दिनों हेतु सुरक्षित)।
  • कोई तृतीय-पक्ष कुकी नहीं: पोर्टल किसी भी विज्ञापन या ट्रैकिंग कुकी का उपयोग नहीं करता।

3. डेटा प्रिंसिपल्स के अधिकार

उपयोगकर्ताओं को अपने डेटा को देखने, सुधारने तथा सहमति वापस लेने का पूर्ण विधिक अधिकार प्राप्त है। शिकायत निवारण हेतु संपर्क पृष्ठ पर संपर्क करें।
