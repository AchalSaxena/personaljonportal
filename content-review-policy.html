Content Review Policy (CRP) सामग्री समीक्षा नीति (CRP)

Compliance Standard: GIGW 3.0 Clause 5.2 (Content Review Policy) • Last Updated: 25 October 2026

The Job Vacancy Intelligence Portal is committed to maintaining content that is current, verified, authentic, and free from obsolete or misleading information. In accordance with Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0), this Content Review Policy establishes structured schedules, roles, and review workflows.

1. Scope and Objective

This policy governs all information published on the portal, including curated job vacancy notifications, candidate credentials for Achal Saxena, compliance statements, external recruitment portal links, and technical metadata.

2. Content Review Matrix & Frequencies

Content Category Review Frequency Review Responsibility Verification Criteria
Job Vacancy Listings Daily / On Scheduler Cycle Portal Administrator Verify application closing dates, official advertisement numbers, notification URL validity, and qualification requirements.
Candidate Credentials & Profile Bi-Monthly or On Role Change Candidate / Achal Saxena Audit credentials (M.Tech CSE, B.E. CSE, NeGD/MeitY, Anthropic AI), experience duration, and target priority locations.
Compliance & Legal Policies (Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer, Accessibility) Quarterly / Upon Statutory Changes Grievance & Compliance Lead Audit conformity with IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023 rules/notifications, GIGW 3.0 amendments, and WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA.
External Links & Configured Sources (job_websites.txt) Weekly Automated Check Automated Health Engine Detect HTTP 404, redirects, domain expiration, or unauthorized URL modifications.

3. Review Procedure & Discrepancy Rectification

  1. Automated Pre-Check: The automated scheduler tests reachability of configured recruitment portals before parsing content.
  2. Content Verification: Vacancy notices are compared against primary sources (e.g., C-DAC, STPI, DIC, RITES, SAIL, NABARD).
  3. Deprecation Handling: Expired recruitment notices past their application closing deadline are marked in accordance with the Content Archival Policy.
  4. User-Reported Errors: Any discrepancy submitted via the Feedback modal or Contact channel is acknowledged within 48 hours and rectified within 5 working days.

4. Contact for Content Queries

For inquiries regarding content accuracy or to report discrepancies, please visit the Contact Us page or use the on-page Feedback tool.
